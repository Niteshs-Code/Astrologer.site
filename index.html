THE ROLE OF ASTROLOGY

Astrology helps us find direction when the path ahead feels uncertain.

As we journey through life, we often keep moving forward without knowing where the path will lead. The planets can provide us with direction and guidance. When we feel lost or confused, the planets and stars can help show us the way. We are not entirely independent, but connected to the planets, stars, and the forces of nature. Astrology offers a way to find answers to the questions within us.

ASTROLOGY — A SCIENCE OF GUIDANCE

Astrology is a science.

Planets • Nakshatras • Cosmic cycles

Astrology is an ancient and profound discipline that explores the influence of planets and celestial bodies on life. Just as the tides of the ocean are influenced by the moon, traditional astrology observes how planetary patterns may correspond with changing experiences and phases of life.

Our sages studied the planets and stars and developed a system through which a person's birth date, birth time and place can be used to create and interpret a birth chart.

The purpose of astrology is guidance. During periods of uncertainty, difficult choices or personal challenges, an astrologer can assess the chart and help a person understand possible patterns, opportunities and areas that require attention.

“Sometimes, we simply cannot understand what is happening in our lives or what we should do next. At such moments, a single piece of guidance can show us the way. A stalled journey can begin moving again. This is the true purpose of astrology — to show you a direction, suggest a path, and light a lamp of wisdom, so that the darkness may slowly fade away.” — Anand
